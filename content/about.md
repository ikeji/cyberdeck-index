# About this site

A link collection of cyberdeck builds and related articles.

- Each entry is a **link to the original post or article**. Use the "View original" button on an entry page to go to the source.
- Images are **cached copies** of the originals.

## Ownership

All posts, articles, images, and other content linked or shown on this site belong to their **original owners and authors**.

## Takedown requests and suggestions

Please send requests through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ikeji/cyberdeck-index/issues):

- **Takedown**: if you are the original owner and would like an image or an entry removed, open an issue with the URL of the entry. It will be handled promptly.
- **Add a deck**: to suggest a build or article for the index, open an issue with its URL.

The source of this site is at [github.com/ikeji/cyberdeck-index](https://github.com/ikeji/cyberdeck-index).
